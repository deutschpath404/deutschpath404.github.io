Planning a Master’s in Germany?

A Master’s application is mostly a comparison: your bachelor’s, your credits, and the prerequisites written on that program page.

We assess

  • Bachelor’s degree
  • CGPA
  • Relevant credits
  • Subject background
  • ECTS requirements
  • Language qualification
  • Work experience
  • Program prerequisites

Subject match comes before the brand name

Two programs with similar titles can ask for different credit groups. A shortlist that ignores that is not a shortlist.

Work experience is sometimes relevant

Some programs ask for it. Many do not. We only treat it as a requirement when the program does.

The route still varies

uni-assist, a VPD, or the university’s own portal — the program decides. We help you follow the one that applies.
