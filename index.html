The situation

Applying to Germany can feel complicated.

German universities can have different admission requirements, application portals, deadlines, document requirements and application procedures.

Students often struggle with:

  • Which university should I apply to?
  • Is my academic background suitable?
  • Do I need uni-assist?
  • Do I need a VPD?
  • Which documents are required?
  • Where should I apply?
  • What is the deadline?
  • How should I prepare for the embassy?
  • What happens after submission?

We help you make sense of the process.

Application routes

One country. Many application routes.

These are routes you may encounter — not a sequence every university follows. The exact path depends on the program.

  1. 01 University Start with the program’s own rules.
  2. 02 uni-assist Only when that university uses it.
  3. 03 My assist The uni-assist applicant portal.
  4. 04 VPD When a preliminary review is required.
  5. 05 University portal Direct application to the campus.
