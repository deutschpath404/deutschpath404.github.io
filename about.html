About

A Germany-only advisory for serious applicants.

DeutschPath helps students from Bangladesh prepare Bachelor’s and Master’s applications to German universities. The work is guidance, organization, and a person you can return to.

What this is

From choosing the right program to preparing your application and embassy documents, DeutschPath helps you navigate your Germany study journey step by step.

The practice is intentionally narrow. Germany only. Bachelor’s and Master’s. Public universities and Universities of Applied Sciences. English-taught and German-taught programs, when they fit the person in front of us.

That focus is the point. A general study-abroad desk tends to reuse the same list. German admissions do not work that way.

Germany only

Every conversation is about German universities, their routes, and the German student visa process.

Clear guidance

You should be able to see what is known, what must be checked, and what nobody can promise.

Human support

Applications raise questions after the form is submitted. Support is meant to continue.

Who we work with

  • Bachelor’s applicants preparing from Bangladesh
  • Master’s applicants comparing program requirements
  • Students who want public universities or Universities of Applied Sciences
  • Applicants who need help reading uni-assist, a VPD, or a university portal
  • Students preparing embassy documents and the online appointment step

What we will not claim

We do not guarantee admission, a visa, an embassy appointment, a scholarship, or a particular result. We do not invent partnerships, success rates, or student numbers.

If a detail is not published on this website yet — a phone number, a fee, a real student story — it is left blank on purpose.
