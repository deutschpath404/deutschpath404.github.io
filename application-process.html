How it works

From profile to Germany

Nine clear steps. Some programs skip or reorder a step — your shortlist decides which ones apply.

  1. Prepare

    1. 01

      Free consultation

      We start with your background and the level you want to study, and separate what is known from what still has to be checked.

    2. 02

      Profile assessment

      Academics, language, and work experience are read against the kind of program you are considering.

    3. 03

      University shortlist

      A short list of programs that may fit — not a list we hand to every student.

  2. Apply

    1. 04

      Application planning

      Which route, which deadline, and which documents belong to each program.

    2. 05

      Document preparation

      Transcripts, certificates, language proof, CV, and motivation letter — only as required.

    3. 06

      Online application

      uni-assist, My assist, a VPD, or a university portal — whichever the program names.

  3. Arrive

    1. 07

      Admission

      Help reading the result. The university makes the decision.

    2. 08

      Embassy preparation

      Document guidance for the student visa file, using the current official checklist.

    3. 09

      Pre-departure

      Practical notes once the plan is confirmed, not before.
