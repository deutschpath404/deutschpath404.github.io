Bachelor’s

Planning a Bachelor’s in Germany?

School qualifications do not map onto German admissions in one universal way. The useful work is finding out what a specific program will actually check.

What we help you understand

  • Academic eligibility
  • School qualifications
  • Subject requirements
  • Language requirements
  • University options
  • Application procedures
  • Deadlines
  • Required documents

Eligibility is program-specific

Some secondary qualifications are compared directly. Some applicants need a preparatory route. That depends on the certificate and the program, so we treat it as something to verify rather than a slogan.

Language is part of the choice

A Bachelor’s may be taught in German, English, or a mix. The program page is the requirement. A general “English is enough” claim is not.

Fees are not assumed

Tuition policies differ by state, university, and your circumstances. We do not describe Germany as automatically tuition-free.
